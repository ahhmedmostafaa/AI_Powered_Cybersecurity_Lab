Results

Key findings

Validated through 240 independent live-attack trials — a live-validated closed-loop hybrid IDS with ML-integrated automated firewall mitigation.

100% Detection Rate
240 out of 240 attacks detected and mitigated across all 8 attack categories, 30 trials each.
872 ms Mean MTTM
Fastest: DDoS/SYN Flood at 743 ms. Slowest: DataExfil at 958 ms requiring the full 120s analysis window.
98.94% ML Accuracy
Macro F1-score 0.9892 across 8 classes. Top feature: flow_packets_s. Lowest: WebAttack F1 = 0.9814.
Zero Dataset Shift
Training data captured from the exact live GNS3/VMware environment — no benchmark generalization gap.
Full 8/8 Coverage
Suricata alone: 6/8. Zeek alone: 4/8. ML alone: 8/8. Combined with layered confirmation: 8/8.
Sub-5ms Firewall Block
SSH-driven pfctl injection into pfSense EasyRuleBlockHostsWAN executes in under 5 ms post-command.
